# Smoke Break brag plan

**What it is.** A pixel-art cigarette pack that floats on everyone's screen. You click it to invite the team to a break. Once two people join, a lighter appears. Lighting up opens Zoom, and the cigarette burns down as the timer.

**Who it's for.** Remote teams who miss the "anyone want to step out?" moment.

**Angle.** Play it straight. It's a real desktop app, and the cigarette is the progress bar. The premise is the joke, so the copy stays dry.

**Hook.** In the first second a cursor clicks the bobbing pack. The lid flips, a cigarette pops up, and a bubble says "Maya wants a 12-min smoke break."

**Highlights**
1. One click invites the whole team. Three screens show the invite landing on each.
2. Two people join, a lighter appears, and lighting up opens Zoom.
3. The cigarette is the timer. It burns down to "Break's over."
4. As a bonus, the video shows the pack and lighter skins.

**Punchline.** "Smoke Break. Lives in your menu bar."

**Tone.** Punchy, playful and clean, with deadpan copy.

**Visual identity**, taken from `src/style.css`:
- The background is the app's fake-desktop gradient, from `#3b4a6b` through `#2a2f48` to `#241f36`, with a 24px grid.
- The colors are ink `#1a1020`, paper `#fffaf0` and red `#d23a3a`.
- Headlines use the project's own 3×5 bitmap font, scaled up. Small lines use SF Mono.
- The real `Scene`, sprites, skins and bubble art draw everything on screen.

**Names on screen** are fictional (Maya, Theo, Sam). The video shows no real teams, codes or links.

**Music.** 120 BPM, so a beat is 0.5s and a bar is 2s. It's soft chiptune in A minor, with a triangle bass, a mellow square lead, a soft kick and hats. Sound effects land on the beat grid. They include pops when cigarettes appear, a lighter click and flame, the catch, a stub-out, the cup filling, and the skin pops.

**Version 2, the slower cut.** It keeps the same music, tempo, effects and skins-on-the-beat ending, but runs 36s instead of 20s so viewers have time to read every bubble and line. It adds the new light-up, where the cigarette is held sideways in the flame, then stands beside the pack, and Zoom opens a beat later. It also adds the water style.

## Storyboard (36.0s, 72 beats)

| # | Time (s) | Scene |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0 to 4 | **Hook.** The pack bobs on the desktop with "Click for a smoke break". The cursor clicks at 2.0. A cigarette pops and the bubble reads "Waiting for takers...". Text: "A cigarette pack that floats on your screen." |
| 2 | 4 to 10 | **Invite.** Three screens show Maya, Theo and Sam. The invite lands at 4.5. Theo joins at 6.5 and the lighters appear. Sam joins at 9.0. Text: "Click it. Your whole team gets the invite." |
| 3 | 10 to 18 | **Light up.** A close-up of Theo's pack. Theo clicks the lighter at 11.5. His cigarette comes out and tips sideways, and the lighter rises. The flame licks it at 12.25. It catches at 12.9 and hops beside the pack. Zoom slides in at 14.0, and tiles land at 14.5, 15.0 and 15.5, when Sam lights up. Text: "Two join. A lighter appears. Light up and Zoom opens." |
| 4 | 18 to 23 | **Timer.** The cigarettes beside the pack burn down. The last minute slows for "Last drag!", and they stub out at 21.0 with "Break's over. Time to hop off Zoom". Text: "The cigarette is the timer." Subtitle: "10–15 minutes. Your team picks." |
| 5 | 23 to 27 | **Water.** The same break plays on Theo's screen as smoke and on Sam's screen as a water cooler. Sam fills a cup at 24.0. Text: "Not a smoker? Switch to water." Subtitle: "Same break. Everyone picks their own style." |
| 6 | 27 to 33 | **Skins.** Seven packs pop on the beat, then eight lighters pop on the eighth notes, with the outro melody rising under the last of them. Text: "Pick your pack. And your lighter." |
| 7 | 33 to 36 | **Outro.** The lit pack, with three cigarettes smoking beside it. The title lands on the melody's peak. Text: "Lives in your menu bar. A Mac desktop app for teams." |

Each cut dips through the background on a beat, taking the old content out and bringing the new content in.
